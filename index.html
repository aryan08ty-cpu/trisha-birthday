A Letter From My Heart

Dear Trisha,

She has been the best person to ever come in my life

One who understands me

Believes me

Trusts me

Assure me when I overthink

Hold me tight when I feel like I have no one

Makes me feel loved

Helps me cope up with my bad mood

Always been there for me after a hectic or a bad day

Whose face lit up with a smile just by my presence

Who feels loved when she is around me

Who fears to lose me

Who gives me time and importance even after so much going on in her life

Who can take my stand even in front of her strict family

Who can trust me enough that even if the entire world is saying something she would still listen to me

Who has always shown me what love feels like

With her I have felt the real meaning of having someone to die for in your life

With her I feel that I have to make a career for myself just so I can live a happy adult life with her

With her I want to travel the entire world

With her I want to experience the birth of my first child

She is a normal girl coming from a normal home but my love for her has made her a goddess in front of me and me as a disciple whose entire happiness comes just by caring for her and serving her so that she could always stay comfortable

This girl is with me when I have no money to take her on expensive dates or gift her expensive things yet a normal day at McDonald's feels like THE MOST Amazing DAY OF OUR LIFE

Never has she complained about anything, she is just happy with my presence and everything that I do for her

I have become the place where she can come to relax when all the evil of the world disturbs her

She has become a chapter of my life whose end is only death

And I have found a purpose in my miserable life that is to keep this girl happy

She looks normal yet beautiful

She is not rich by money but by heart

She stays serious yet I have the loudest laughs with her

She has anger issues yet only gets comfortable with me

She is a girl yet she has become my everything...

She is my Trisha

Forever Yours,
Aryan ❤️
